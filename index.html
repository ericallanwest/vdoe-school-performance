About the data

Performance: VDOE statewide SPSF results, 2025-26 (framework score, category, federal identification). Locations: NCES EDGE public school file, 2024-25. Income: ACS 2024 5-year, table B19013, census tracts.

Attendance zones are approximations, not official boundaries: each division's territory is split among the schools serving each grade by nearest school (Voronoi), and zone income is the household-weighted average of the tract medians it overlaps. Real zones follow roads, rivers and policy, and magnet/virtual schools draw division-wide.
